Seismic reflection survey design — interactive widget

Assumptions (explicit):
  • Symmetric split-spread about each shot (geophones centred on the shot).
  • Maximum offset = distance from shot to outermost geophone = floor(N_channels/2) × geophone spacing.
  • Maximum depth of useful reflection imaging ≈ 0.5 × maximum offset (rule-of-thumb).
  • Fold is computed by simulating all shot–geophone traces and binning midpoints along the line.
  • Roll-on calculation: if available physical geophones < required channels, we compute how many forward moves (roll-ons) are needed per shot to cover the spread.
